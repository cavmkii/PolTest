What the percentages mean: "Accept or lean toward P: 50% (40%)" means that
50% of respondents endorsed this answer inclusively (including multiple-answer
endorsements) and 40% endorsed this answer exclusively (excluding multiple-answer
endorsements).
These results should not be used for comparison to 2009 results due to different
populations and parameters. For meaningful longitudinal comparisons, see the
longitudinal analysis section.
